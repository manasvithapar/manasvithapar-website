Privacy & YouTube API notice

How this website handles source-linked media and third-party services.

Last reviewed: 3 November 2026. This notice applies to manasvithapar.com and the source-linked media archive on this website.

Information the site displays

The Media archive links visitors to original publisher pages and Manasvi Thapar’s official YouTube channel. The site does not ask visitors to sign in with a Google or YouTube account, and it does not collect YouTube passwords or account credentials.

YouTube API Services

When the managed official-channel catalogue is enabled, this website uses YouTube API Services to refresh limited public metadata for videos from the official Manasvi Thapar channel: the video title, public YouTube URL, video identifier and YouTube publication time. Each such record is visibly attributed as “YouTube · Official channel upload.” The website does not use this data to infer viewing behaviour, create audience profiles or access private videos.

YouTube and Google handle information under their own policies. Please review the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service. Any YouTube-powered feature on this website is provided subject to those terms.

Third-party embeds and links

This website may link to or embed services such as YouTube, X, LinkedIn and external news publishers. Those services may collect information or set cookies under their own policies when you follow a link or view an embed. Their practices are not controlled by this website.

Retention and accuracy

If the managed official-channel catalogue is enabled, its public YouTube metadata will be refreshed on a defined schedule and removed or updated when it is no longer available. A date labelled “Official upload” is a YouTube publication time; a date stated in a video title is not presented as a broadcast date. The site does not copy or re-upload broadcaster footage or articles.

Corrections and questions

To request a correction to a source-linked record or ask a privacy question, use the contact page. Requests are reviewed against the original publisher or platform source.
