Manasvi Manu Thapar

Manasvi Thapar is an Indian advocate and entrepreneur. From legal practice to public talks and media conversations, his journey has been shaped by ideas, learning and action.

Candour Legal identifies him as an advocate and founder. TED records his February 2018 TEDxHLCC talk, “What others don't teach you, you teach yourself.”

This page brings together a few moments from that journey. For interviews, appearances and original coverage, visit the media page.
