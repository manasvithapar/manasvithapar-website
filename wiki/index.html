BIOGRAPHY

Meet Manasvi Thapar

Lawyer · Entrepreneur · Thought Leader · TEDx Speaker · Angel Investor · Mentor

“Believe it!
Be it!”

Manasvi Thapar is the founder of Candour Legal – a first of its kind legal firm offering complete legal solution under one roof;

He is the Co Founder & Mentor of Cubix Tech Solutions which is a P2P gaming platform.

He is Chairman & Mentor of Coined.ch – World’s Leading Decentralized Invest-Gaming Platform.

Founder of Indus Mentors – India’s First & Largest Network of Mentors, providing mentorship support to startups and SMEs;

Co-Founder of Lothal Venture LLP – a pan India Angel Investors Network investing & supporting new age technologies & startups;

Manasvi Thapar is the grand son of renowned sanskrit scholar Dr. (Prof.) Mahendra Pratap Thapar & great grand son of Shri. Sardari Lal Thapar who was a freedom fighter & fought the initial elections from Bharatiya Jana Sangh from Hoshiarpur, Punjab.

He is the Director of Karnavati Club Ltd, Global Hospitals, City Pulse Multiplex Ltd.

He is the Vice President of Gujarat State Chess Association. He was a national player for chess, representing Punjab.

He served as the Co-Chairman of CII Gujarat Taskforce for Startups from 2016-2020. He was the Chairman of Entrepreneurship & Innovation at Young Indians, Ahmedabad.

He is well known for his socio-political activities. He one of the most respected and viewed voice on National News Debates across India.

He is the founder of Startups & Entrepreneurs Federation.

He was also the Executive Director & Mentor of Tapinnonvations which have created a shoe for the blind people which shows the way to them.

Former President of Analog and Digital Labs – a Traffic Intelligence and system provider.

He has launched a massive Youth movement to promote Entrepreneurship and Startup Culture, with Startups & Entrepreneurs Club around the nation.

He has kick-started an online campaign know as www.fileRTI.org which enables people to file RTI online.

He was the District Rotaract Representative of Rotary International District 3051 for the year 2014 – 2015.

He is on board of a dozen of startups & has mentored 150+ startups. Thapar has worked as a visiting lecturer at a various prominent colleges & has been invited as guest speaker on several occasions at various States, National universities & International Forums to motivate & inspire the youth on various diverse topics.
